About

How it works. Face Rater finds landmark points on your face, turns your head back to face the camera and compares the layout with the model faces. The closest one counts, and each part of the face gets its own score too. It is a fun comparison, not a real measure of beauty.

Photos are processed on this device and never uploaded.

The leaderboard is optional. If you opt in, your nickname, score and region scores are stored with Supabase (EU). Nothing else, and never a photo.
